# StoryLens Format Experiment

## Decision

The next proof of concept will test a minimalist 2D historical-explainer format inspired by the communication mechanics of a reference video that received close to one million views.

The view count is useful market evidence, but it does not prove that art style alone caused the performance. Topic, title, thumbnail, hook, narration, channel history, distribution, and viewer satisfaction may all have contributed. StoryLens will therefore test the full format as a package rather than merely imitate the line art.

## What to adopt

- An immediate second-person hook
- Conversational narration with light observational humor
- One main idea per visual
- Direct synchronization between words and pictures
- A meaningful visual change every 3–5 seconds
- Simple reusable characters, props, poses, and backgrounds
- Visible action and expression rather than slideshow-style camera movement
- Clear subtitles that support, rather than dominate, the image

## What StoryLens adds

- A sourced historical foundation
- Explicit separation of evidence, interpretation, and reconstruction
- Respectful portrayal of capable ancient people
- Place-specific environments and material culture
- Selective atmospheric scenes, environmental sound, and emotional tension

## What not to copy

- Exact character designs or silhouette language
- Individual compositions or shot order
- Script wording, jokes, titles, or thumbnails
- Branding, fonts, subtitle treatment, or recurring visual gags

## Sample success criteria

The revised 25–30 second sample should contain 6–9 distinct visual ideas and score at least:

- 8/10 for immediate clarity
- 8/10 for narration–visual synchronization
- 8/10 for pacing and visual variety
- 7/10 for human-authored feel
- 7/10 for atmosphere
- 8/10 for historical credibility

It must also be materially easier to produce and revise than the first sample. After the internal comparison, the stronger version should be tested with real viewers before the permanent channel format is locked.
