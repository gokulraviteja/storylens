# Pilot 001 Retrospective

Date: 2026-08-31

## Experiment

Produce a complete English video about Mesolithic hunter-gatherers living through a central Indian monsoon using a researched script, generated illustration boards, synthetic narration, burned-in subtitles, ambience, and a code-driven edit.

The preserved production project is stored outside this repository at `StoryLens-Projects/001-stone-age-monsoon/`.

## Outcome

Technically complete; creatively rejected.

The result demonstrated that the workflow could produce a sourced, coherent, and exportable video. It did not meet the desired standard for audience attention or human-authored character.

## What worked

- Strong separation of evidence and reconstruction
- Useful claim ledger and historical guardrails
- Visual inspection caught tool and grinding-stone errors
- Reproducible audio timing, subtitles, scene preparation, and export
- Consistent broad palette and setting

## What failed

- Forty-nine shots across 4:33 averaged roughly 5.6 seconds per shot and often reused similar compositions.
- Generated frames looked recognizably AI-made despite stylistic consistency.
- Motion depended mainly on cutting between static frames.
- The 871-word narration was accelerated to roughly 191 words per minute and felt uniformly synthetic.
- Music and designed sound were too limited to create an emotional arc.
- The narration evaluator awarded a perfect score even though the finished performance did not hold attention.
- The workflow proceeded to a full render before a representative finished opening had been approved.

## Reusable lessons

1. Require a finished 45–60 second prototype before full production.
2. Evaluate voice by listening, not by reading the script or checking timing.
3. Plan substantially more meaningful visual beats, while keeping individual frames simple.

## Changes made

- Added the prototype gate.
- Added a line-utility and anti-AI writing standard.
- Added directed sectional narration and listening tests.
- Added visual-cadence ranges and pattern changes.
- Added sound hierarchy, music transitions, device testing, and selective captions.

## Not concluded

The experiment does not establish that synthetic narration, generated illustration, or the minimalist format can never work. It establishes that this particular combination and execution did not work well enough.
